What we verify

Four pillars of complete due diligence

📍

Location & Zone

CDP zone classification, lake buffer (75m/150m), NGT tribunal orders, AAI airport buffer, proximity to water bodies and forests — everything that affects what you can build.

📋

Revenue & Documents

RTC/Pahani, full mutation register (ownership chain), 30-year Encumbrance Certificate from Kaveri, Khata, property tax receipts — the complete legal document trail.

⚖️

Legal & Court Cases

eCourts API (district + HC + SC), BDA acquisition notices, layout approval status, RERA registration, lake/water body encroachment flags — every active legal risk.

👤

Owner Background

Aadhaar/PAN linkage, CCTNS criminal records (via AuthBridge), CIBIL flag, joint ownership check, and full 30+ year ownership chain from Bhoomi mutation history.
